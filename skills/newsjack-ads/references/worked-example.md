# Worked example: three newsjack videos for a cohort course (September 2026)

<!-- Before publishing this package: the course owner confirms this example may be public. It contains no account
     identifiers, budgets beyond "small", performance data or participant stories. -->

**Product:** "AI + Системное мышление", a six-session Russian-language cohort course on systems thinking with AI, for
founders, managers, product and engineering leads. Sales window: early price until 19 Sep 23:59 UTC; start 3 Oct.
**Audience reads:** English tech press and Russian-language AI Telegram channels.
**Reference:** "Foldable since 1843", a spec ad (never run by its brand).

## What the run produced

| | Story (dates) | Shape | Property (course data) | Final video headline / punch | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fish | AI lab heads publicly propose to slow the race (12 Sep; 4 outlets) | Rivals agree to restrain themselves | Session 5 game «Рыболовство»: teams share one ocean they cannot see into; 13 rounds; why an agreement without control stays a wish | «Главы трёх AI-лабораторий: давайте притормозим.» ("Heads of three AI labs: let's slow down.") / «Проверим за 13 раундов.» ("Let's test it in 13 rounds.") | 19 Sep 16:59 PT (the early price ends) |
| Rehire | Forecast: 30% of staff cut for AI will be rehired by 2029 (analyst firm, 9 Sep; 3 outlets) | A fix reversed after a delay | Session 2 archetype "shifting the burden" | «Сократили людей ради AI.» ("Cut people for AI.") / «Не новость. Архетип «сдвиг бремени».» ("Not news. The 'shifting the burden' archetype.") | 3 Oct |
| Bottleneck | Energy named the constraint on AI data-centre build-out (2-8 Sep; 3 outlets) | "The bottleneck is now X" | Session 6, Goldratt's five focusing steps; a March webinar had traced chips -> memory | «Узкое место опять переехало.» ("The bottleneck moved again.") / «Сняли ограничение? Ищите следующее.» ("Removed a constraint? Look for the next.") | 3 Oct |

Each video, ~16-18 s, silent: the news line over a text-free illustration on frame 0 -> the structure on the course's
own object (the game's harbour map with fleets sailing out; a causal diagram drawing itself on a photographed sheet; a
red circle moving along "chips -> memory -> energy" on a whiteboard) -> the punch -> a cream end card with a bridging
lead ("Why an agreement without control stays a wish, we take apart in the course"), the course name and dates.

An A/B variant of each replaced the illustration with generated footage of the news scene (robots racing, an office,
a data centre), chosen by watching a side-by-side comparison of four footage models.

## What changed between the report and what ran

- The first brief asked for static ideas; the owner asked for video because an image did not carry the link to the
  news.
- The review gate (two vendors) rewrote the statics: the fish headline had to contain the news word ("slow down",
  not "catch less"); the diagram's delay mark was on the wrong link; a named person left the sign-off; the webinar
  example and September's news were separated in copy; stop dates moved to the day each line stops being true.

## What an external review found afterwards (lessons for the next run)

- **The review covered the statics; the final videos were never reviewed.** Run the publication gate on the exported
  files.
- **The course name arrives at 14-15 s.** A viewer can watch almost all of it without learning what is sold or why it
  matters to them. Beat 2 now names what is sold, and the brief starts from the buyer's decision.
- **The fish story included outside verification** in the proposal, while the ad's lesson was "an agreement without
  control stays a wish". Sourced story + sourced property is not enough: list where they differ.
- **The rehire primary text ran to three sentences plus a homework line** against a 1-2 sentence rule.
- **Three stories per ad set** meant the illustration-vs-footage comparison could not be read per story.
- **A same-week second scan** re-found all three live stories, because nothing marked them as taken, and spent 40
  minutes rebuilding the property bank nobody had saved.
