# Lessons from real newsjack runs

- **Name the format as an open choice in the brief.** A first run produced static ideas because the brief said "static
  ad ideas"; the reference was blamed until the log showed otherwise. An image alone did not carry the link to the
  news (the viewer had to rebuild the story), so video became the default.
- **A sub-agent's news report is a lead, not a source.** Re-check stories in the main session before any build.
- **Some sites block automated fetches** (seen Sep 2026: reuters.com, businessinsider.com, qz.com, openai.com blog
  pages, mathandai.org, help.x.com, mlq.ai, androidheadlines.com; tomshardware.com returned only its paywall). Wire
  stories can often be read through syndication (Reuters on investing.com, Bloomberg on Yahoo Finance), which counts
  as the same source, not a second one. Search across 2+ newsrooms, and record which pages were opened.
- **The report's copy will not survive the build unchanged.** One review moved the news word into the headline
  ("slow down" instead of "catch less"), split a historical example from today's news that shared one unlabeled
  drawing, and moved stop dates: an ad that says "from 3 October" stops on 3 October; a price deadline in UTC means an
  earlier stop in the Americas.
- **Three copy defects two independent reviewers both caught** on a second run: a news line a cold viewer read as the
  product's own news; a product claim narrowed when quoted; primary texts of 3-4 sentences against a 1-2 sentence rule
  (the live ads had the same defect).
- **The two-second read cannot be verified from text.** Both reviewers said so. Only a person who has not seen the
  brief can tell: show the final cut and ask them to retell the joke.
- **A same-week second scan re-finds the stories that are already live.** Without the "taken" status in the bank it
  wastes the run on them.
- **The Russian-language channels decided a pick that English aggregators barely ranked.** "Where the audience reads"
  is an input, not a detail.
- **Launch shape that worked operationally:** 3 stories x 2 news-beat variants (illustration vs generated footage), one
  ad set per variant, equal small budgets, cloned from a proven ad set; all ads cleared review in about 10 minutes. It
  cannot isolate illustration vs footage per story: three stories per ad set let delivery pick different stories in
  each arm (see ad-test-loop).
- **A strong joke on a 9-day-old story went to reserve:** by production time it had 3-4 days left.
- **The best-looking property can overclaim.** A webinar named one bottleneck months earlier; the news named another.
  The ad may say the bottleneck moved, not that the product predicted the news.
- **Check the reference's provenance.** A spec ad proves shareability, not conversion.
